# NovaShip Logistics - Claims, Payments and Support

## Lodging a complaint
Call the helpline or e-mail support with your tracking number. Complaints receive a
reference number and a response within 24 hours.

## Claims for loss or damage
Claims must be filed within 7 days of the booking date with photos of the parcel and
packaging. Shipments are insured up to Rs 10,000 by default; declared-value insurance
of 2% covers higher-value parcels.

## COD payments
COD amounts are transferred to the seller's bank account on the same day the parcel is
delivered. Sellers can view payment status in the NovaShip seller portal.

## Right of inspection
NovaShip may open and inspect any parcel to check for prohibited items.

## Support hours
Phone and e-mail support is available 9 am to 9 pm, seven days a week.

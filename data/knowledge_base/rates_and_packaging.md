# NovaShip Logistics - Rates, Weight and Packaging

## Rates (per shipment)
| Service | Up to 0.5 kg | Each extra 0.5 kg |
|---|---|---|
| Same-day (metro) | Rs 250 | Rs 60 |
| Overnight | Rs 300 | Rs 80 |
| Economy (overland, min 3 kg) | Rs 450 for 3 kg | Rs 50 per kg |

## Volumetric weight
Charges use the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight.
Volumetric weight (kg) = length x width x height (cm) / 5000.

## Packaging
Use a sturdy carton or flyer. Liquids must be sealed and double-bagged. Fragile items
need bubble wrap and a "Fragile" label. NovaShip sells flyers and cartons at every
branch.

## Items we do not carry
Cash, jewellery, firearms, explosives, flammable liquids, drugs, live animals and any
item prohibited by law.

# NovaShip Logistics - Services and Coverage

> NovaShip is a fictional courier company used as demo data for this project.

NovaShip delivers parcels for online sellers and individuals across 800+ cities in
Pakistan, with same-day delivery inside Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad/Rawalpindi.

## Services
- **Overnight**: next-day delivery between major cities, booked before 6 pm.
- **Same-day (metro)**: within the same city if booked before 1 pm.
- **Economy (overland)**: 3-5 working days, cheapest option for heavy parcels.
- **Cash on delivery (COD)**: we collect payment from the receiver and transfer it to
  the seller on the same day the shipment is delivered.
- **Fulfilment and warehousing**: storage, picking and packing for e-commerce sellers.

## Delivery attempts
A courier makes up to three delivery attempts. After the third failed attempt the
parcel is returned to the shipper and return charges apply.
